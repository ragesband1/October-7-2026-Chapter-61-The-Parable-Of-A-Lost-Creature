📖 The Parable Of A Lost Creature

Chapter 61: Bedtime Stories — April 10, 2020

Once upon a time in the town of Omegapo, a man named Indi-Ego felt profoundly bored. Seeking companionship, he visited the pet market and purchased an omnivorous pig for $500.

The owner gave a stern warning: "I have only 1 condition. You need to care of the pig and discipline its childish brain. If you leave it without discipline, the hungry and starving townspeople will view it as a meal!"

Overcome by laziness, Indi-Ego took a nap at 3 PM. Awakened at 6 PM by strange jolly noises, he discovered his beloved pet had roamed outside and been slaughtered by the gluttonous crowd.

Moral Lesson: If you get what you want, don't forget to protect and discipline it from harm. Laziness breeds regret, and unaddressed pride leaves wounds that can never be undone.
